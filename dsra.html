Drones & the rules

How drones help

  • 🌡️A thermal camera picks up body heat, so a person or a dog in long grass, gorse or woodland edges shows up as a bright shape.
  • 🗺️Fields, woodland edges, farmland and shoreline can be checked in minutes rather than hours on foot.
  • 🌙Works best at night and early morning, when the ground is cool.

Thick trees and buildings can block the view, so drones work alongside searchers on the ground, not instead of them.

Safety & the rules

  • ✈️Our pilots fly under UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) rules.
  • 🚫Some areas, such as near Glasgow Prestwick Airport, need permission before we can fly.
  • 👮For a missing person, we only fly when Police Scotland have asked us or agreed.
  • ✋Seen something during a search? Tell the team or the police straight away. If it’s a dog, please keep back and don’t try to catch it.
  • 🛑Please don’t fly your own drone near a search. Two drones in the same sky is dangerous, and our pilots would have to land.
