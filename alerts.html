Missing dog alerts

Get a notification on your phone when a dog goes missing near you.

On iPhone, add us to your home screen first

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open LDA from your home screen, tap Missing dog alerts, and turn them on there.

Alerts can’t be set up in this browser

Please open this page in Chrome or Safari on your phone. On iPhone you need iOS 16.4 or later.
