Worried about a dog’s welfare?

If you have reason to believe that a dog is injured, neglected, being mistreated or in distress, but isn’t loose or lost, please contact the Scottish SPCA.

Scottish SPCA

The Scottish SPCA is Scotland’s animal welfare charity. Its inspectors are authorised by the Scottish Government to act on animal welfare, and it works in partnership with Police Scotland.

Animal helpline, open 9am – 5pm, every day. Concerns can only be reported by phone.
